## 3.1. User-Facing Features (Epic/User Story Format)

### Epic 1: User Authentication (MVP)

- **Story 1.1**: As a new user, I want to connect my HashPack wallet so I can interact with the platform.
- **Story 1.2**: As a connected user, I want to see my wallet address and HBAR balance displayed in the app.

### Epic 2: Goal Management (MVP)

- **Story 2.1**: As a Goal-Setter, I want to fill out a form to create a new goal, specifying a title, duration (7-30 days), and HBAR stake (1-100 HBAR).
- **Story 2.2**: As a Goal-Setter, I want to view all my "Ongoing," "Completed," and "Failed" goals on a personal dashboard.
- **Story 2.3**: As a Goal-Setter, I want my dashboard to show my progress, including which days I have submitted proof for and their verification status.

### Epic 3: Proof & Verification (MVP)

- **Story 3.1**: As a Goal-Setter, I want to upload a daily proof (image or text) for my ongoing goal.
- **Story 3.2**: As a Verifier, I want to browse a queue of submitted proofs that are awaiting verification.
- **Story 3.3**: As a Verifier, I want to stake 1 HBAR (`VOTER_STAKE`) to cast a "Yes" or "No" vote on a proof's validity.
- **Story 3.4**: As a Verifier, I want to earn `VOTE_POINT`s for each valid vote I cast.

### Epic 4: Economic & Rewards (MVP)

- **Story 4.1**: As a Goal-Setter, I want my full stake returned to my withdrawable balance if my goal is finalized as "Completed."
- **Story 4.2**: As a Goal-Setter, I want 75% of my stake returned to my withdrawable balance if my goal is finalized as "Failed."
- **Story 4.3**: As a Verifier, I want my 1 HBAR `VOTER_STAKE` returned to my withdrawable balance after the goal I voted on is finalized.
- **Story 4.4**: As a user, I want to withdraw my entire available HBAR balance from the platform to my connected wallet with a single transaction.
## 3.2. Smart Contract Specifications (StakeIt.sol)

### File: stake_it.sol

- **Solidity Version**: ^0.8.20

### Imports

- `@openzeppelin/contracts/access/AccessControl.sol`
- `@openzeppelin/contracts/security/ReentrancyGuard.sol`
- `@openzeppelin/contracts/security/Pausable.sol`

### State Variables & Constants

- **Roles**: `ADMIN_ROLE`
- **Treasury**: `charityWallet`, `platformWallet`, `liquidityReserve` (all `address immutable`)
- **Economic Constants**:
  - `MIN_STAKE`
  - `MAX_STAKE`
  - `MIN_DURATION` (days)
  - `MAX_DURATION` (days)
  - `VOTER_STAKE`
  - `PENALTY_PERCENT` (e.g., 25)
  - `PLATFORM_FEE_PERCENT`, `CHARITY_FEE_PERCENT`, `LIQUIDITY_FEE_PERCENT` (detailing the penalty split)
- **Points**: `VOTE_POINT`, `COMLETION_POINT`

### Data Structures

- `struct Goal { ... }`
- `mapping(uint256 => Goal) public goals`
- `mapping(address => uint256) public withdrawable`
- `mapping(address => mapping(uint256 => uint256)) public voterStakes`
- `mapping(address => mapping(uint256 => mapping(uint256 => bool))) public voterVoteBitmask`
- `mapping(address => uint256) public userPoints`

### Functions (Public/External)

- `createGoal(uint32 _durationDays, ...)`: (Payable) Creates a new goal, stakes `msg.value`.
- `castVote(uint256 goalId, uint26 day, bool isValid)`: (Payable) Stakes `VOTER_STAKE`, records the vote, and awards points.
- `finalizeGoal(uint256 goalId)`: (Public) Automatically finalizes a goal after duration expires, aggregates votes on-chain, and triggers fund distribution based on majority vote.
- `withdraw()`: (Non-reentrant) Withdraws caller's `withdrawable` balance.
- `refundVoterStake(uint256 goalId)`: (Non-reentrant) Refunds the caller's `VOTER_STAKE` for a finalized goal. (Note: This may be automated in finalization.)
- `pause()` / `unpause()`: (Admin only)
- `grantRole()` / `revokeRole()`: (Admin only)

### Events

- `GoalCreated(...)`
- `VoteCast(...)`
- `PointsAwarded(...)`
- `GoalFinalized(...)`
- `FundsDistributed(...)`
- `Withdrawn(...)`
- `StakeRefunded(...)`
