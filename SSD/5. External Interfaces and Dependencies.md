## 5.5. Decimal Handling Specification (CRITICAL)

This is a critical integration point that must be handled correctly.

- **Hedera (HBAR) Native**: 8 decimals (tinybar).
- **EVM (via JSON-RPC)**: 18 decimals (weibar).
- **StakeIt.sol Contract**: Constants must use 18 decimals for EVM compatibility.

### Resolution (Mandatory Requirement)

- The StakeIt.sol contract uses 18 decimals exclusively.
- All constants are defined with 18 decimals (e.g., `MIN_STAKE = 1 * 10**18`).
- The frontend must scale values from wallet decimals (8 for HashPack) to 18 decimals before sending to the contract.
