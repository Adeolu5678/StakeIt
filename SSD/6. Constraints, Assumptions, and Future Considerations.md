## 6.1. Technical Constraints

- **Solidity Version**: ^0.8.20.
- **Verification**: The MVP relies on a "wisdom of the crowd" voting model, which is vulnerable to collusion (though the `VOTER_STAKE` mitigates this).
- **On-Chain Aggregation**: Vote aggregation is performed on-chain, requiring efficient gas usage for large numbers of votes.

## 6.2. Assumptions

- Users will have a basic understanding of Web3 wallets and HBAR.
- The `ADMIN_ROLE` keys will be managed with high security.
- Users will act in good faith (e.g., not spam proofs or votes).
- On-chain vote aggregation will be sufficient for goal finalization without external intervention.

## 6.3. Future Considerations (Post-MVP Roadmap)

- **AI Verification**: Introduce an AI/ML model to automatically verify proofs (e.g., "is this a photo of a gym?"). This would reduce reliance on manual voting.
- **DAO Governance**: Transition the `ADMIN_ROLE` to a DAO. The community could then vote on parameters like `PENALTY_PERCENT` and `charityWallet` address.
- **NFT Rewards**: Integrate HTS to mint a "Proof of Completion" NFT for users who successfully complete their goals, creating a non-transferable, on-chain record of their achievements.
- **Gamification**: Fully tokenize the `userPoints` system into a tradable HTS token, creating an in-app economy and leaderboard system.
